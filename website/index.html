A time-blocking planner and time tracker for Mac, Android and Nextcloud

How it works

  1. 1

    Commitments

    What you give time to: Thesis, Work, Teaching. Each one gets its own color and its own calendar.

  2. 2

    Blocks

    Every event in a commitment's calendar is a block of time for it. Drag to plan them; repeat them every weekday or every week.

  3. 3

    Tasks fill the blocks

    Tasks queue up per commitment, with subtasks and estimates like "Draft 45m". Each block takes tasks from the top until it's full; what you don't finish moves to the next block.

  4. 4

    Press play

    Track time with one tap, on the Mac from the menu bar too. Forgot to start? Move the start back. Done and Time show your week as it really was.

A calendar made for blocks

Day, week and month. Drag to create, move or resize a block; click one to see the tasks waiting in it. Use the built-in calendar, Apple Calendar or your Nextcloud calendars.

Everything for focused weeks

Now

Today as a timeline, the running timer, and only the tasks for this block.

Weekly goals

Hours per week for each commitment, with progress on the Now page.

Tasks and estimates

An ordered queue per commitment. Check off subtasks in order; it warns you when the tasks need more time than the blocks have.

Notes

Notes per commitment in Markdown, with checklists you can tick.

Pomodoro and reminders

Optional Pomodoro rounds, and a reminder before a block ends.

Done and Time

Your week on a timeline: planned blocks, tracked time and what you checked off. Hours per commitment, with CSV export.

Your data stays yours

No accounts with us, no tracking, no ads. Blockwise works offline and syncs only where you choose.

Nextcloud

On your own server. Blocks are events in Nextcloud Calendar; Mac, phone and web stay in sync.

Dropbox · Google Drive

Mac only. Synced through your own Dropbox or Google Drive, end-to-end encrypted with a passphrase only you know.

This Mac only

No sync at all. You can connect a sync later and your data comes along.

Mac

Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later

Download for Mac (.dmg)
How to install
  1. Open the .dmg and drag Blockwise onto Applications.
  2. The app isn't notarized by Apple yet. The first time, open it, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Android

Android 8 or later · needs a Nextcloud

Download for Android (.apk)
How to install
  1. Open Blockwise.apk on your phone; allow your browser to install apps if asked.
  2. Log in to your Nextcloud. Blocks come from the phone's calendars: sync your Nextcloud calendars with DAVx⁵.

Nextcloud

Nextcloud 32 to 35, with the Calendar app

Download the Nextcloud app
How to install
  1. Extract it into your server's apps folder: it makes a folder called tracker.
  2. Enable Blockwise under Apps, or run: occ app:enable tracker
